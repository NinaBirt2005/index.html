The World of Herons

A closer look at these long-legged birds

About Birds

Birds are warm-blooded vertebrates that have feathers, beaks, and wings. Most birds can fly, although some species cannot. They live in many different habitats around the world and have different diets and behaviors.

Herons are birds in the family Ardeidae. They are known for their long legs, long necks, and pointed bills. Many herons live near lakes, rivers, marshes, and other wetland habitats where they hunt for food.

Three Types of Herons

1. Great Blue Heron

The great blue heron is a large wading bird found throughout much of North America. It has blue-gray feathers, long legs, and a long, pointed bill. It often stands still in shallow water while searching for fish and other prey.

Read more about the Great Blue Heron

Characteristics

  • Habitat: Lakes, marshes, and coastal areas
  • Food: Fish, amphibians, and other small animals
  • Appearance: Blue-gray feathers and a long neck

2. Grey Heron

The grey heron is a tall wading bird found across parts of Europe, Asia, and Africa. It has gray feathers, a pale head, and long legs. It usually lives near water and hunts by waiting patiently for prey to come within reach.

Read more about the Grey Heron

Characteristics

  • Habitat: Wetlands, rivers, and lakes
  • Food: Fish, frogs, and small animals
  • Appearance: Gray body, pale head, and long legs

3. Green Heron

The green heron is a smaller heron found in North and Central America. Its feathers have dark green coloring, and its neck and chest have rich chestnut coloring. Unlike larger herons, it often hunts from the edge of the water instead of wading far into it.

Read more about the Green Heron

Characteristics

  • Habitat: Ponds, marshes, and wooded waterways
  • Food: Fish, insects, and amphibians
  • Behavior: Often waits quietly to catch its prey
